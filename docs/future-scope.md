# Future Scope

These are not implemented in Phase 12:

- Multilingual resume and job-description processing.
- Stronger locally hosted language models with documented evaluation datasets.
- Enterprise ATS and calendar integrations.
- Email notifications with an organization-managed mail service.
- Multilingual recruiter Copilot.
- A richer skill ontology and synonym governance workflow.
- Learning-based ranking only after validated, legally reviewed datasets exist.
- Multi-worker rate-limit storage and enterprise deployment controls.
- Expanded compliance reports and retention policies.
