# 15-Minute Demo Script

1. **Minute 1:** State the manual recruitment problem and SmartHire's objective.
2. **Minute 2:** Show the architecture: browser, Flask, services, local AI, SQLAlchemy, Socket.IO.
3. **Minute 3:** Sign in as the fictional demo recruiter and show the dashboard KPIs.
4. **Minute 4:** Open a job and show structured skills and requirements.
5. **Minute 5:** Open JD analysis and review extracted skills, experience, education, and responsibilities.
6. **Minute 6:** Open a candidate and show parsed contact, skills, projects, and experience.
7. **Minute 7:** Open ranking and point out actual component scores and recommendation tiers.
8. **Minute 8:** Open one candidate's explanation and score trace; show matched and missing skills.
9. **Minute 9:** Shortlist a candidate and show the notification/activity update.
10. **Minute 10:** Schedule an interview, demonstrate conflict validation, then show the interview list.
11. **Minute 11:** Open Analytics and explain funnel, trends, skill gaps, and job performance.
12. **Minute 12:** Ask Copilot: “Show top candidates” and “What are the recruitment insights?”
13. **Minute 13:** Sign in as admin and show system analytics, AI audits, and health checks.
14. **Minute 14:** Explain CSRF, RBAC, upload validation, fairness, and human oversight.
15. **Minute 15:** State measured test results, limitations, and future scope.

Reset the fictional dataset before a presentation with `python scripts/reset_demo.py --yes` in a non-production environment.
