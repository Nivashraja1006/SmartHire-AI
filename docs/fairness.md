# Fairness and Human Oversight

SmartHire uses job-relevant skills, experience, education, certifications, projects, application data, and semantic relevance. It does not intentionally use gender, religion, caste, race, ethnicity, marital status, political affiliation, appearance, photographs, or inferred protected attributes.

Recommendations and scores are decision-support outputs, not autonomous hiring decisions. Recruiters must review evidence, missing data, organizational policy, and applicable law before acting. Candidates can see a transparent explanation of the job-relevant score factors without seeing confidential recruiter data.
